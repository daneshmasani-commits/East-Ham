About the practice

A family practice rooted in East Ham, caring for generations of local families

East Ham Dental Care is a family-run dental practice on Barking Road, looking after the families of Newham and East Ham for generations. Run by the Deo family and led by Principal Dentist Dr Tajinder Deo, we have grown into a close, friendly team while keeping the personal service our patients know us for.

Many of our patients have been with us for decades — some first came as children and now bring their own little ones through our doors. We offer both NHS and private treatment, so whether you need a routine check-up or more specialised private care, we can help.

We treat patients of all ages and take particular care with those who feel nervous or anxious about visiting the dentist. Your comfort, and your family's trust, matter to us.

    Private: from £120

  • Treatment for gum inflammation and periodontal disease, which if left untreated can lead to tooth loss. We clean below the gumline and give you a clear plan to get your gums healthy again. Early treatment really does make a difference.

  • Restorative treatments to rebuild strength and fill gaps where teeth are damaged or missing. A crown caps and protects a single tooth, while a bridge replaces one or more missing teeth for a natural look and feel. We'll talk you through the best option for your situation.
